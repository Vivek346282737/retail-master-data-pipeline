# Business Case & Executive Analytics Framework

## 1. Problem Statement
Multi-vendor catalog ingestion introduces duplicate SKU collisions, unstandardized naming, and pricing margin inversions (Cost Price > Selling Price), causing an estimated 3.8% direct financial revenue leakage across retail operations.

## 2. Core Business Hypotheses
- **H1 (Data Quality Drift):** Over 10% of supplier feeds contain whitespace formatting defects or duplicate SKUs.
- **H2 (Pricing Anomaly Risk):** High-ticket furniture items (CAT-FURN) exhibit greater margin risk due to un-indexed vendor pricing updates.

## 3. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV):** Aggregate revenue across validated catalog assets.
- **Data Quality Index (DQI):** Percentage of raw records successfully achieving 'Golden Master' status.
- **Leakage Prevention Value:** Cumulative financial loss blocked via automated margin violation flags.
